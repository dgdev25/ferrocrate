# S178 — project volume with a bind specification: `no such volume: <project>-<name>`

- suite: compose-e2e · class: product defect · status: closed · first seen: 2026-08-28
- command: `docker compose -f compose.yaml up` where a top-level volume has `driver_opts: {type: none, o: bind, device: ...}` (TestProjectVolumeBind/up_on_project_volume_with_bind_specification)
- Docker: creates the project volume with the bind options and mounts it.
- Ferrocrate: exit 1, `Error response from daemon: no such volume: compose-e2e-project-volume-bind_project-data` — the volume was never created (or was created without registering the name).
- impact: 1 failure.
- regression tests: `volume create` with driver_opts bind, assert `volume ls`/`inspect` show it; compose `up` over a project-volume bind fixture.
- fix: project-scoped Compose volumes now retain their declared driver and bind options during prerequisite creation.
