- area: workspace test harness (`tests/common/`) · class: test · status: open · first seen: 2026-08-29
- command: any consumer of `tests/common/runner.rs` (e.g. `TestRunner::run()`), and inspection of `tests/common/isolation.rs`.
- Expected: the custom runner registers 10 suites (`ferrocrate_test_suites()`, runner.rs:145) with `parallel`, `fail_fast`, and `timeout_seconds` config, implying it executes them.
- Actual: `TestRunner::run()` (runner.rs:88) filters suites by priority/category, sorts, then returns `self.results.iter().collect()` — `results` is never populated. - scope: misleading only — no test is skipped, because nothing consumes the runner. Risk: a future change wires it in believing timeout/retry/isolation semantics exist; readers of tests/common assume parallelism or guard cleanup that does not happen.
- test: n/a (this is the test harness itself). Fix is either: (a) delete the dead runner/guards and document cargo/libtest as the harness, or (b) implement execution. (a) is the honest minimum; the suites-by-name registry has no executors to run.
