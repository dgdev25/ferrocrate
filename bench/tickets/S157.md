# S157 — Dockerfile heredoc bodies are parsed as top-level instructions
- suite: buildkit-dockerfile (at least 28 direct product failures) · class: product · status: closed · first seen: 2026-08-28
- command: `GO_TEST_TIMEOUT=0 SUITE_SRC=/data/dev/bench-suites bash bench/suites/run-suite.sh buildkit-dockerfile --engine ferrocrate`.
- Docker: `RUN`, `COPY`, and `ONBUILD` heredocs retain their body through parsing, including indentation, variable substitution, special symbols, and shebang/fake-heredoc forms. Ferrocrate: reports `unsupported Dockerfile instruction: instruction ECHO/BEGIN/FOO/OK/\"QUOTES is not supported`, then downstream expected output files do not exist.
- cause: the Dockerfile parser terminates the instruction before consuming the heredoc delimiter/body, so body lines re-enter the normal instruction dispatcher. This is one parser-state defect spanning all listed heredoc spellings.
- scope: Dockerfiles using BuildKit heredoc syntax in `RUN`, `COPY`, or `ONBUILD`; it also masks later exporter and provenance assertions that depend on the produced file.
- test: parse and build a minimal `RUN <<EOF` body plus `COPY <<EOF` and `ONBUILD RUN <<EOF`, including an indented delimiter and a shebang body; assert that no body line is treated as an instruction.
- evidence: fresh failures include `TestRunBasicHeredoc`, `TestRunFakeHeredoc`, `TestRunShebangHeredoc`, `TestHeredocIndent`, `TestHeredocVarSubstitution`, `TestCopyHeredoc*`, and `TestOnBuildHeredoc` on all worker/frontend variants.

Fix: heredoc delimiters and bodies are consumed as one logical Dockerfile instruction; RUN and COPY bodies are retained and materialized.
